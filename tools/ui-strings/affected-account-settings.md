## Known limits

- The corpus is a snapshot from 2026-10-31, not the live KB.
- Only en-US source is searched.
- A text that an article paraphrases, or splits with markup such as `{menu}` or a link, does not match.
- A cell with several texts lists all the texts that changed together for one setting, so the new text for one old text is among them, not always beside it.
