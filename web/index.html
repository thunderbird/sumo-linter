    Everything runs in your browser. Your markup is never uploaded, so unpublished drafts stay private.

    House style normalises headings to whichever style the article already uses most, so a consistent article is left exactly as it is. Nothing is rewritten just to look tidy — every source change is reviewed by volunteer localizers.

    Only safe fixes are applied — repairs whose intent is a guess are reported but left alone, because most markup errors have several plausible corrections and picking wrong silently changes what an article says.
